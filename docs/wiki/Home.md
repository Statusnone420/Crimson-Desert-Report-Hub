# Crimson Desert Report Hub

Crimson Desert Report Hub is an unofficial fan newspaper with original reporting, selected press and creator coverage, a patch desk, and a player-report board. Editorial stories, player evidence, check-ins, scanner leads, and official claims stay distinct.

The public site is designed to remain useful with zero visitors. Quiet means quiet; it never becomes proof that a problem is fixed.

## The public promise

- Reports are evidence, not a raw public complaint dump.
- Player taps are signals, not identity or consensus.
- Scanner links are leads, not automatic proof.
- Official notes provide context, not a guarantee of player outcomes.
- Editorial selections retain source attribution; scanner discovery cannot automatically publish them.
- No accounts, email collection, ads, or analytics trackers.
- Reddit API access is permanently off.
