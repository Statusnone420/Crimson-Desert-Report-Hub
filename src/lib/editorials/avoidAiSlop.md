## Pre-publish checklist

1. Every factual claim traces to Hub data or a linked official or primary source. No invented quotes, numbers, or "players are saying."
2. Attribution names the source and the count ("3 Steam threads"), never "many players" or "experts."
3. No inflated significance: no "testament," "pivotal," "landscape," "marks a shift."
4. No promo adjectives (vibrant, rich, groundbreaking, boasts, renowned).
5. Scan for AI vocabulary. If three or more turn up, rewrite those sentences.
6. "Is/has" in place of "serves as/stands as/features."
7. No "not X but Y" unless X is a real belief someone holds. No reflexive triplets.
8. No "-ing" tail clauses that add meaning nobody sourced.
9. No hedging boilerplate ("details are limited," "based on available information"). Say what we checked.
10. No chatbot chatter, placeholders, brackets, `XX` dates, or tool artifacts (`oaicite`, `[cite:`, `grok_card`, `utm_source=`).
11. Formatting check: sentence-case headings, minimal bold, no emoji bullets, no bold-label lists, no stray Markdown in X posts.
12. Em dashes kept rare. Commas, periods, and parentheses do most of the work.
13. No formula ending ("Despite these challenges...", "In conclusion"). Stop when the content stops.
14. Every link clicked and working.
15. Read it aloud. Press release or help-desk reply? Rewrite.
